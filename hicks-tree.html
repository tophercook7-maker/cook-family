The wall — one generation back

Alfred's attached parents in the shared tree — Elijah Hicks (1790, North Carolina) and Elizabeth Farmer — don't add up: the record has the mother born in 1800 (she'd have been 14 at Alfred's birth) and the couple marrying in 1822, after Alfred was already born.

So that link is broken — Alfred (1814, North Carolina) is the honest edge of the Hicks line until better records turn up.

The Journey

North Carolina (Person County, late 1700s) → the north-Georgia hills (Habersham, Cherokee, Gordon — Resaca and Demorest, early-mid 1800s) → Arkansas (Polk County, by the 1870s–80s).

Four generations to Alfred (1814). The same north-Georgia country the Brocks crossed — two families who would meet, generations later, in the Arkansas hills.
