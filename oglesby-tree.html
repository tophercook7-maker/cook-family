The Direct Line — five generations

Oldest at the top. Laura Elizabeth Oglesby married Benjamin Franklin Cook — their nine children are the Arkansas Cooks, and their son Lewis was Topher's grandfather. Vitals checked on FamilySearch (Aug 2026).

The Irish branch — through the McCains

Lewis Green's wife, Elizabeth Bernetta McCain, opens a whole second overseas line: → Hance McCain Jr. (1797 Guilford Co., N.C.; died at Hot Springs, Arkansas, 1847) → “Senator” Hance Hamilton McCain (1763 Virginia, buried at Buffalo Presbyterian Church, N.C.) → Lt. William McKean, born 1704 in Ballymoney, County Antrim, Ireland, who sailed to Pennsylvania in 1722.

A Scots-Irish (Ulster) line — the name spelled both McKean and McCain. William the immigrant is well-documented; the generations above him are disputed among McKean researchers, so we stop at Ballymoney. (Family lore even ties to Thomas McKean, a signer of the Declaration — but that exact link isn't proven.)

The Journey

Scotland (Annandale, Dumfriesshire) → through the port of Charleston to Newberry District, South Carolina (1790s) → Tuscaloosa, Alabama (1830s) → North Carolina / Texas → Arkansas (Pulaski → Scott → Polk) → Laura born in Indian Territory, Oklahoma.

Five generations, ~1751 to today — with an Irish branch reaching back to 1704, and the Savage line (below) coming down instead from the North, out of Illinois.
