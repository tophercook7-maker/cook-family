The Journey

Virginia (1730s) → North Carolina (1780s) → Georgia, the Habersham & Gilmer hill country (1800s–1820s) → Arkansas, Polk County (from the mid-1800s to today).

Eight generations, roughly 1734 to now. The Brock side reaches further back than the Cook side does — all the way to colonial Virginia.
